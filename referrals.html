Who we serve

Built for the clinicians who refer to us.

Therapists & Counselors

When a client you're seeing needs a psychiatric evaluation or medication support, we can take on the medication side while you continue their therapy. We coordinate with you, with the patient's consent.

  • Psychiatric evaluation and diagnostic clarification
  • Medication management for depression, anxiety, ADHD, PTSD, bipolar, and more
  • Telehealth anywhere in Washington State
  • A consistent provider for each patient, no rotating clinicians
  • Referring clinician notified when the patient is scheduled

Primary Care Providers

How it works

Simple referral. Fast follow-through.

We designed the referral process to be as light as possible on your end. Submit the form, and we take it from there.

1

Submit the referral

Fill out the short form below with your contact details. No patient information goes through the website. Takes under a minute.

2

We call you, then the patient

Within 24 hours, our intake team calls you to collect the patient's details securely by phone, then contacts the patient directly to schedule their evaluation.

3

You're kept in the loop

You receive confirmation once the patient is scheduled. From there, our psychiatric provider manages their care and keeps you informed on significant changes.
