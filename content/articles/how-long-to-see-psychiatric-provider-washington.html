If you've tried to find a psychiatrist in Washington, you've probably run into long waits, full schedules, or practices that don't take your insurance. You're not imagining it. There aren't enough psychiatric providers for everyone who needs one, especially outside the Seattle area. The good news is that there are ways to get seen faster.

Why the wait can be long

Many psychiatric practices have more patients than they can schedule, and some don't accept new patients at all. Wait times vary a lot depending on where you live, your insurance, whether you need a specific specialty, and whether you're willing to do visits by video. In rural parts of the state, the nearest in-person provider can be hours away.

Ways to get seen sooner

  • Be open to telehealth. If you're located in Washington during your visit, you can see a provider anywhere in the state. That opens up far more options than searching only near home.
  • Consider a psychiatric nurse practitioner. PMHNPs evaluate, diagnose, and prescribe independently in Washington. Read more about psychiatric NPs vs. psychiatrists.
  • Check your plan's network first. Calling practices that are in network saves time. See the plans we accept.
  • Ask for a cancellation list. Many practices fill openings from a short list of people who can come on short notice.
  • Lean on your primary care provider in the meantime. They can often start treatment or bridge your medications while you wait.

If you can't wait

If things feel urgent or you're having thoughts of harming yourself, don't wait for an appointment. Call or text 988, go to the nearest emergency room, or call 911.
