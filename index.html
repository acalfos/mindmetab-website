Getting started

Three steps to feeling better

We've made it as easy as possible to go from "I need help" to having a care plan in your hands.

01

Request care

Fill out a short intake form online. Tell us what you're going through. Share as much or as little as you're comfortable with. No commitment required.

02

Meet your provider

Connect with a board-certified psychiatric provider through a secure telehealth visit or in-person appointment. They'll listen, ask questions, and begin understanding the full picture.

03

Start your care plan

You'll leave your first appointment with a clear, personalized plan. Ongoing support, medication management if needed, and regular check-ins.

Conditions we specialize in

Our practice is built around the conditions that most affect your ability to live fully. We serve teens and adults (ages 13 and up). If you don't see your concern listed, reach out. We may still be able to help.

Anxiety & Panic Disorder

Constant worry, racing heart, sudden panic attacks. We help you understand what's driving it and build real tools to regain calm and control.

Bipolar & Mood Disorder

Mood swings that leave you and the people around you exhausted. We help you find a treatment that holds, so the highs and lows stop running your life.

Depression

When everything feels heavy and joy is hard to find. We listen, ask the right questions, and work with you on a plan that actually helps, not just adjustments that get you by.

ADHD

Difficulty focusing, staying organized, or finishing tasks. ADHD shows up differently for everyone. We treat teenagers and adults with personalized strategies.

PTSD

Trauma doesn't have to define your future. We work with you at your pace to help you get out from under what happened and feel safe in your own life again.

Schizophrenia

Dementia & Cognitive Decline

We provide psychiatric support for patients and families navigating dementia, memory loss, and cognitive changes, helping manage behavioral symptoms and improve quality of life.

Insomnia & Sleep Disorders

Elizabeth Calfos

DNP, PMHNP-BC · Psychiatric Mental Health NP

Dr. Calfos earned her Doctor of Nursing Practice from George Washington University and is board-certified in psychiatric mental health. She takes time with every patient. She asks the questions other providers skip, listens without rushing, and builds a care plan that actually fits your life.

Her experience spans anxiety, depression, bipolar disorder, ADHD, trauma, and women's mental health, including pre- and postpartum care. She provides psychiatric evaluations and medication management for adults and seniors.
